# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Reading Pack Bot to be respectful, constructive, and
safe. Contributors and maintainers should be able to discuss technical choices
without harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific and constructive when discussing code, documentation, and ideas.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Accept correction, acknowledge mistakes, and keep disagreement focused on
  the work.
- Protect private information and follow the repository's security and data
  handling rules.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, sexualized attention, discriminatory
language, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained
disruption are not acceptable. Do not use project spaces to target people or
to publish credentials, private logs, transcripts, manuscripts, or deployment
identifiers.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other spaces where someone is representing this project.

## Reporting

Send a private report through GitHub's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/ktakahashi74/reading-pack-bot/security/advisories/new).
That channel accepts conduct reports as well as security reports; begin the
title with `Conduct report`. Do not put sensitive details in a public issue.

Maintainers will review the available evidence, limit disclosure to people who
need it, and give affected people a reasonable opportunity to respond. Reports
made in good faith will not be penalized.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban a
participant when necessary to protect the project and its community. Decisions
should be proportionate, documented privately when appropriate, and based on
the behavior rather than the person's identity or viewpoint.
